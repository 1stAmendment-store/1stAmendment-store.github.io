Movie Magic 8 Ball

Try it in your browser. Pick your streaming services, shake for a movie, and keep track of your Collection and what you've Watched.

Demo download is about 30 MB.

  • Guest demo. No sign-in. It includes Home, Collection and Watched; the full app adds themes, friends, chat and more.
  • Your lists stay in this browser. They're kept on this device only, and clearing your browser data removes them.
  • 5 shakes an hour in the demo, Try Again included.

Movie data from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Streaming availability from JustWatch.
